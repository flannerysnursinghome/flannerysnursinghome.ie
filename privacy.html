Privacy notice

How we handle personal information when you use this website, ring us, or send us an enquiry.

Who we are

Flannery's Nursing Home Limited, Chapel Road, Abbeyknockmoy, Tuam, Co. Galway, H54 PX79. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us on 093 431 30 or at info@flannerysnursinghome.ie.

What this website collects

Nothing, unless you send it to us. The forms on this site do not store anything on a server. When you press send, they open your own email app with the details filled in, and you send the message yourself from your own account.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics on this site. Our typefaces are served from our own server, so your visit does not generate a request to Google or anyone else.

What we receive when you contact us

If you ring, email, use a form or write to us, we receive what you choose to tell us: your name, contact details, the person the enquiry is about, and something about their circumstances. Enquiries about a nursing home often include information about health, which is special category personal data.

We use it to answer your enquiry, to arrange a visit, and, if you go on to apply for a place, to assess whether we can care for the person safely. Our lawful basis is your consent, and, where the enquiry leads to admission, the provision of health and social care.

Job applications

Applications for jobs are made through Connecteam, our staff management system, which handles them on our behalf. The form asks for your name, contact details, CV and, for nursing posts, your NMBI registration. We use that only to assess your application. We keep unsuccessful applications for no longer than twelve months.

If you make a complaint

Complaints are handled under our complaints procedure, and are recorded separately from any resident's care record. Those records are kept for at least four years. We may need to share details with the person the complaint is about and with anyone investigating on our behalf.

Who we share it with

Only where it is necessary: with the HSE in relation to the Fair Deal scheme, with a resident's GP or other treating clinicians, with our professional advisers, and with a regulator where the law requires it. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to admission are kept only as long as they are needed, and no longer than two years. Records relating to residents are kept in line with our records retention policy and the Regulations that apply to nursing homes.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where the law allows, or object to how we are using it. To do any of that, contact us at the address above.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, or through dataprotection.ie.
