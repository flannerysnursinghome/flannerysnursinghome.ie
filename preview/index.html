A day here

The day, roughly.

Nobody is on a timetable they did not agree to.

Morning

Tea, the paper, the radio

Waking is unhurried. Staff are on the floor through the night.

Midday

Dinner at the table

Cooked fresh, with an assisted dining room at the pace of the person eating.

Afternoon

Something on, nothing forced

Music, bingo, flower arranging. In fine weather the path gets used.

Evening

Mass, then the quiet hours

Mass is celebrated weekly in the house; night staff come on.
