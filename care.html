Our care

We accommodate men and women aged 18 and over who need nursing care. Care is individualised and planned with the resident and their family, and reviewed as needs change.

  1. 01

    Residential care

    A full-time home from home for the majority of our residents, who along with their families have direct input into their care, activities and surroundings. Our commitment is to a physical environment built around the individual needs of each resident.

  2. 02

    Respite care

    Supporting you by supporting your caregiver. We know the commitment and the hard work given by carers at home, and we offer temporary care so a carer can take a break, recharge, and keep going for longer.

  3. 03

    Convalescence

    Recuperation and recovery after surgery or serious illness. A stepping stone back to home life and daily activities, with physiotherapy input available to help in your recovery.

  4. 04

    Palliative care

    Maintaining quality of life through the prevention and management of pain and other physical and psychological symptoms. We work with the specialist palliative services in Galway, and we keep family close.

  5. 05

    Dementia care

    Care planned around the person, at the pace the day allows. Our Activities Coordinator is trained in the Sonas programme, Ireland's leading therapeutic activity for people living with dementia.

Nursing care, around the clock

We are staffed by registered nurses and healthcare assistants, day and night, with the number and skill mix appropriate to the needs of the people living here. GPs from the surrounding hinterland continue to care for their own patients in the home, and we work with them to make sure the right package of care is in place for each resident.

Every bedroom, en-suite, corridor, communal room and exit door has a nurse call point, and every nurse carries an internal cordless phone.

Fees and the Fair Deal scheme

Many people who come to live here do so under the Fair Deal scheme, the State's Nursing Home Support Scheme. Under it, you pay a contribution based on your income and assets, and the State pays the balance directly to the nursing home.

We will talk you through what it means in your own case, and help with the application if you would like that. Ring 093 431 30 and ask for the person in charge.

Applications for Fair Deal are made to the HSE, not to us. Your local HSE Nursing Homes Support Office handles the assessment.

What to bring to the conversation

The person's current medication list, who their GP is, and a rough idea of what they need help with day to day. That is enough to start.

How admission usually works

Every situation is different, but this is the shape of it.

  1. Ring or send an enquiry

    Tell us who it is for and what is happening. We will tell you honestly whether we can help.

  2. Come and visit

    Walk the building, meet staff, see a room. Bring whoever you would bring.

  3. Assessment

    We meet the person and review their needs, so we can be sure we can care for them safely.

  4. Fair Deal application

    If the scheme applies, the HSE assesses your contribution. We can help with the paperwork.

  5. Move in

    We agree a date, and plan the first weeks together, with the family involved.

Not sure whether now is the right time?

Most families who ring us are not sure. Ring and describe the situation; we will give you a straight answer, even if it is that you are not ready yet.
