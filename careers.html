Work with us

A family-run nursing home in Abbeyknockmoy, with a close-knit team, ongoing training and room to grow. We take applications all year round, whether or not a particular role is advertised.

Roles at Flannery's

Each role has its own short application form — name, phone, email and your CV, a few minutes on a phone. Send one whenever suits; we review them as roles come up.

  • Healthcare Assistant

    Full and part time, day and night duty.

    Apply for Healthcare Assistant

  • Staff nurse

    Day and night duty. The form asks for your NMBI registration.

    Apply for Staff nurse

  • Activities coordinator

    Planning the weekly programme and organising outings and events.

    Apply for Activities coordinator

  • Chef

    Fresh cooking daily in our own kitchen, for residents and staff.

    Apply for Chef

  • General Assistant

    Housekeeping, laundry and support around the house.

    Apply for General Assistant

  • Manager / Admin Roles

    Office and administrative roles. Tell us what you are looking for.

    Apply for Manager / Admin Roles

Applications go into Connecteam, our staff system. If you would rather talk to someone first, ring 093 431 30 and ask for the person in charge.

What it is like to work here

We are a small home, which means you know every resident and every colleague. You will see the difference you make on the same day you make it.

We place a strong emphasis on ongoing training and staff development, and we support staff to take on more responsibility as they are ready for it.

Students and placements: ask about availability by ringing the number above.

Before you apply

Garda vetting applies to all roles working with residents. We will explain what is involved before you are asked for anything.

Want to see the place before you apply?

Ring and arrange a look around. It is easier to decide once you have stood in the day room.
