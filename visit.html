Arrange a visit

Ring us, or send the form below, and we will arrange a time that suits. You can also use the form to ask about a bed, respite, or joining the waiting list.

Come and see it

Come when it suits you, morning or evening. Ask to see a room, the day rooms, the dining room and the garden. Ask the staff anything you like; they will tell you the truth, including the parts that are not glossy.

Bring the person who may be moving in, if they are able to come. And bring whoever else is part of the decision.

Getting here

Take the N17 to Tuam. After Claregalway, turn right onto the N63 Roscommon road for Abbeyknockmoy, about ten miles. Go through the village for about a mile, turn right at the school and past the church. Flannery's is about a mile on the left.

Request a visit or ask about a bed

We cannot confirm a bed online. What we can do is ring you back as soon as we can, and talk it through.

How should we reply?
Or ring 093 431 30

Sending this opens your own email app with the details filled in, addressed to us. Nothing is stored on this website. How we handle your details is set out in our privacy notice.

What happens after you ring

  1. We listen

    Who it is for, what is happening at home, and what you are worried about.

  2. We tell you straight

    Whether we can meet the need, and when we might have a bed. If we cannot help, we will say so and point you elsewhere.

  3. You visit

    At a time that suits, with whoever you want to bring.

  4. Assessment and paperwork

    We meet the person, review their needs, and help with the Fair Deal application if that is the route.

Prefer to talk it through first?

Ring and ask for the person in charge. There is no script and no sales pitch; we will tell you what we can and cannot do.
