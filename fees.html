Fees and the Fair Deal scheme

Most long-term nursing home care in Ireland is paid for through Fair Deal, the State's Nursing Home Support Scheme. Here is how it works, in plain terms.

What Fair Deal is

Under the scheme, the HSE assesses what you can afford to contribute from your income and assets. The State pays the balance directly to the nursing home. You are not asked to find the full weekly fee in cash.

The assessment looks at income, savings and property. There are rules that cap what is taken from a home, and provision for a spouse or partner who remains at home. The HSE does that assessment, not us.

What we do

We will talk you through what it means in your own case, and help with the application if you would like that. Ring 093 431 30 and ask for the person in charge.

If a person is coming to us for a short stay rather than long-term care, that is arranged differently. Ask us and we will explain.

What to have to hand

A list of the person's income and pensions, their savings and accounts, and details of any property. If there is a spouse or partner at home, their details matter too.

The order of things

You apply to the HSE, not to us. The usual sequence is: visit the home, have an assessment of care needs, apply to the HSE for Fair Deal, then agree an admission date once approval comes through.

Applications and assessments are handled by your local HSE Nursing Homes Support Office. We are glad to help with the paperwork, but the decision is theirs.

The usual route in

Every situation differs, but this is the shape of it.

  1. Ring or send an enquiry

    Tell us who it is for and what is happening. We will say honestly whether we can help.

  2. Come and visit

    Walk the building, meet staff, see a room. Bring whoever you would bring.

  3. Assessment of needs

    We meet the person and review what care they need, so we know we can care for them safely.

  4. Fair Deal application

    The HSE assesses your contribution. We help with the forms and answer what we can.

  5. Move in

    We agree a date and plan the first weeks together, with family involved.

Not sure what it would cost you?

Ring and ask. We will explain how the assessment works and what to expect, without any obligation.
