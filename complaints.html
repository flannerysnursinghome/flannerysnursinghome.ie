Complaints and feedback

If something is wrong, we want to hear it. A complaint will not count against you or the person you are speaking for. We publish this page because we are required to publish our complaints procedure on our website.

Who can complain

A complaint can be made by a resident, a spouse, civil partner, cohabitant, close relative or carer of a resident, a person who has the care of the resident's affairs by law or court appointment, a legal representative, or anyone else with the resident's consent.

How to make one

Any of these is fine. There is no form you must use and no particular words.

  • Tell us in personAny member of staff. Ask for the person in charge if you would rather.
  • Ring us093 431 30, and say you want to make a complaint.
  • Write to usChapel Road, Abbeyknockmoy, Tuam, Co. Galway, H54 PX79, marked for the complaints officer.
  • Email usemail us with “complaint” in the subject line.
  • Use the form belowIt sends to the same place, and you can print it instead if you prefer paper.

What we will do

A complaints officer, who is not involved in the matter you are complaining about, will investigate. We will conclude the investigation as soon as possible, and no later than 30 working days after we receive your complaint. You will get a written response telling you whether the complaint was upheld, the reasons, any improvements we recommend, and how to seek a review.

If you are not satisfied

You can ask for a review. A review officer, again someone not involved in the matter, will review the decision and conclude it as soon as possible, and no later than 20 working days after receiving your request. You will get a written response with the outcome.

If we need longer

If either timeline cannot be met, we will tell you in writing when you will hear from us, and why it is delayed.

Going further, and getting help

You do not have to handle it alone, and you can take it outside the home.

Independent advocacy

If you would like someone independent to help you make a complaint, or to understand the process, we will help you find that support. Sage Advocacy provides a free and independent service for older people.

Sage Advocacy

The Ombudsman

If you are not satisfied after our review, you can bring the complaint to the Office of the Ombudsman, which is independent of us and free to use.

ombudsman.ie

HIQA

HIQA is the regulator, not a complaints service for individual matters, but it does want to hear about concerns about the quality and safety of care in a centre. You can raise a concern with HIQA directly.

Your centre's page on hiqa.ie

Our records

Every complaint, the investigation, the outcome and any action we took are recorded, separately from the resident's care plan, and kept for at least four years. You can ask to see what we hold about your complaint.

Send a complaint or a comment

You can also use this form to send a compliment or a suggestion. Use the form below, ring us, or write to us; whichever is easier.

Consent

Sending this opens your own email app with the details filled in, addressed to us. Nothing is stored on this website. If you would rather not use email at all, ring us, call in, or post a letter.
